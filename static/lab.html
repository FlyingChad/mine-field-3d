Head-to-head match

Every game puts all the ticked models (2–6) on one board at once. A win goes to the highest score, and ties share the win.

Add a bot to a game room

Bots join as real players with a character. They walk, dig and flag under the same reach and stun rules as people. Difficulty sets how quickly they act. Bots leave when the last human leaves the room.
